*Quantum Theory* is a study circle in which covered a brief review on the pragmatic meaning and an extensive discussion on foundations of quantum mechanics. It is desired to entirely cover the topics of a book written by [Asher Peres](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/0-306-47120-5). Here's the content I wish to cover by the end of the circle:
**Part I:** \*Introduction to Quantum Physics \*Quantum Tests \*Complex Vector Space \*Continuous Variables
**Part II:** \*Composite Systems \*Bell's Theorem \*Contextuality
**Part III:** \*Space-time Symmetries \*Information and Thermodynamics \*Semiclassical Methods \*Chaos and Irreversibility \*Measuring Process. You can also see the playlist of these set of lectures in [my youtube channel](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLc4X8NbutFknJXG5cV_7WLPPd6GCiIDoa&si=zHxW-VeS5VYW5dZW).
